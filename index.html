        Which flux?

        Agglomerated fluxes matched to our SAW wires. Supplied in 25 kg bags and drums.

        Wire and flux pairing (AWS SFA 5.17)

        WireFlux SFNFlux SFHFlux SFS
        EL8F7AZ-EL8F6A0-EL8F7AZ-EL8
        EM12KF7A0-EM12KF7A2-EM12KF7A4-EM12K
        EH14––F7A4-EH14

        Weld-metal values depend on the flux. EL8 and EM12K pair with SFN, SFH and SFS; EH14 with SFS. For Mo-alloyed wires (EA2, EA3, EA4), use a basic flux and ask us for the right combination. Store flux dry; re-dry at 300–350 °C for 2 hours if the bag has been opened or exposed to humidity.
